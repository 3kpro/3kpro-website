STAGE 01 // ENTRYSTANDALONE: $550

The Wave™

Stabilizes Incoming Airflow & Raises Vacuum

Machined wave-form geometry on the high-pressure side stabilizes incoming airflow before it enters the throttle body, dropping the pressure curve and raising intake manifold vacuum so atomized fuel vaporizes for complete combustion.

STAGE 02 // VELOCITYSTANDALONE: $250

The Cyclone™ 🌀

Controlled Helical Vortex-Flow Motion

Specially designed vortex-flow pathways machined into the bore introduce a turbulent rotational charge of motion to maintain kinetic energy and accelerate air velocity straight into the intake runners.

STAGE 03 // IGNITIONSTANDALONE: $250

Port & Polish

Zero-Restriction Mirror Bore Finish

Precision CNC/hand porting and mirror polishing removes casting restriction at the point of ignition, increasing CFM and allowing conditioned air to enter the intake path with minimal boundary drag.

FLUID DYNAMICS // HOW THE WAVE™ WORKS

Vortex Generator Machining Inside the Throttle Bore

As laminar intake air passes the butterfly valve, it encounters our precision-machined wave groove in the throttle body wall. On every intake stroke, this geometry sheds coherent vortex spirals that elevate manifold vacuum, turning liquid fuel droplets into vapor for the fastest combustion rate possible and "Hair Dryer-like" clean exhaust.
