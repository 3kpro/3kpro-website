HOW WAVE TECHNOLOGY WORKS

A SIMPLE CONCEPT. POWERFUL RESULTS.

WAVE technology uses precisely engineered waveforms and air-vortex enhancement to improve the way air and fuel mix inside your engine. By machining patented geometric grooves across the throttle body bore and butterfly valve trailing edges, incoming air forms high-velocity micro-vortices on every intake stroke—atomizing fuel droplets for a more complete combustion process, better performance, improved mileage, and reduced emissions.

Paired with Tribo-MAX™ Boron-Based Nano-Ceramic surface restoration (which bonds to friction-heated metal surfaces and lubricates 12× better than motor oil alone), your engine recovers lost compression, vacuum, and throttle snap.
